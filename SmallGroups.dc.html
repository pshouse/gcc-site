This fall's study

ACTS: The Work of the Holy Spirit

The Spirit moved in dramatic ways in the early church. The gospel was preached, thousands were added to their number, many were healed, and the good news about Jesus spread across continents. Covering the exciting action of the Spirit in the years just following Jesus' life on earth, this study will open your eyes to the Spirit's power in the past and present.

Study guide: Acts: Seeing the Spirit at Work by John Stott
